Signature Services

Every treatment is tailored with surgical precision using advanced professional gear and pH-balanced products.

1. Premium Car Wash

Multi-stage foam bath, wheel decontamination, and streak-free blow drying ensuring absolute zero swirl marks on your luxury paintwork.

2. Interior Cleaning

Comprehensive leather conditioning, dashboard sanitization, and air vent sterilization for a showroom-fresh, pristine cabin environment.

3. Carpet Shampooing

Hot-water extraction and anti-bacterial foam treatments to eliminate deep-seated dust, stubborn stains, and allergens from vehicle mats and carpets.

4. Scratch Removal

Precision rotary and dual-action machine polishing to safely eradicate surface scuffs, swirl marks, and unsightly blemishes from your clear coat.

5. Paint Restoration

Multi-stage paint correction process that revives factory-grade depth, mirror gloss, and vibrant pigment clarity for showroom excellence.

6. Hand Wax Application

Premium carnauba wax sealants meticulously hand-applied to provide incredible hydrophobic water sheeting, deep gloss, and robust UV defense.
