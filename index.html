Signature Services

Every treatment is tailored with surgical precision using advanced professional gear and pH-balanced products at Lakshmi Autocare.

1. Denting

Expert precision body panel reshaping, dent removal, and structural alignment to restore your vehicle's factory form to absolute perfection.

2. Painting

Factory-grade computerized color matching, dust-free paint booth baking, and immaculate finish coats for a breathtaking brand-new sheen.

3. Washing

Multi-stage foam wash, wheel decontamination, and streak-free blow drying ensuring absolute zero swirl marks on your luxury paintwork.

Advanced mechanical diagnostics, engine tuning, and comprehensive mechanical repairs performed by certified expert technicians.

High-precision 3D computerized wheel alignment and balancing to ensure optimal steering stability and even tire wear.

6. AC Service

Comprehensive climate control servicing, gas top-up, leak testing, and evaporator cleaning for powerful cabin cooling performance.
